# AllOnFire Video Overlay Product

register: product

## Product Purpose

AllOnFire produces Italian AI-tool explainer videos from source material, transcripts, and local rendering tools. The overlay system creates visual aids for moments where spoken explanation benefits from structure: dashboards, terminal output, flows, comparisons, decision matrices, and concept maps.

## Users

- The channel owner, who reviews overlay ideas before turning them into Remotion templates.
- Future implementation agents, who translate approved visual mockups into reusable video overlay components.
- Italian viewers interested in AI workflows, local automation, coding assistants, and practical tool usage.

## Audience Mood

The viewer is watching a technical explanation, often on a laptop or phone, and needs the visual aid to reduce cognitive load quickly. The overlay should feel precise, useful, and a little sharp, not decorative or generic.

## Strategic Principles

- Design mockups first, convert to Remotion later.
- The visual system should support both 16:9 long-form video and 9:16 shorts.
- Overlays are information-bearing, not b-roll.
- Reuse one theme across many overlay archetypes.
- Prefer clean product UI over editorial YouTube graphics.

## Anti-References

- Generic SaaS cards with repeated icons.
- Neon AI dashboards on pure black.
- Decorative glassmorphism.
- Thumbnail-style shock graphics.
- Terminal-only visuals for every concept.
